# Persist workout-local exercise order immediately

Exercise reordering in active logs and completed-log editing saves immediately so the chosen sequence survives reopening, including template exercises without results and newly added unlogged exercises that participate in the reordered workout. This extends the persistence boundary in [ADR 0002](0002-persist-workout-exercise-replacements.md): preserving those additions is necessary to restore the saved sequence, while unrelated draft edits retain their existing save behavior. Moving an exercise preserves its identity, sets and recorded details, leaves the source template unchanged, and restores the previous order with a retryable error if saving fails.

Workout logs and templates use the same reorder appearance and interaction. Existing templates also save exercise moves immediately, preserving required exercise membership and planned set data without implicitly saving unrelated draft edits; new templates save their initial sequence on creation. Failed template moves restore the previous order with the same error and retry behavior, and template reordering does not rewrite already persisted workout-local sequences.

Both editors support drag-and-drop alongside accessible up/down arrow controls. A completed drop uses the same immediate persistence and rollback behavior as an arrow move; cancelled drags and drops that do not change the sequence do not save.

Dragging shows a compact floating preview with the exercise name and set count, a placeholder and insertion line, and neighboring exercises shifting to reveal the proposed position. Holding near the top or bottom of the workout's scroll area auto-scrolls that area, increasing speed nearer the edge and stopping when the pointer or finger leaves the edge region, the drag ends, or it is cancelled; drop geometry must remain accurate while scrolling. Mouse and touch use the same behavior in both editors, movement animations respect reduced-motion preferences, and dropping uses the shared saving and success feedback in [ADR 0004](0004-consistent-save-feedback.md).

After a position-changing button move or completed drag, immediately reveal the moved exercise in its new position while saving continues, aligning its header and reorder controls near the top of the workout viewport with a small margin, subject to scroll limits, even if it was already partly visible. Use fresh layout measurements, preserve its expanded or collapsed state, and reveal it again at its restored position if saving fails. Reveal scrolling is smooth normally and immediate with reduced motion; a newer move replaces an ongoing reveal, manual scrolling or beginning another drag cancels it, and cancelled or unchanged drags do not trigger a reveal.
